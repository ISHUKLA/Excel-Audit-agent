# Independent challenge author brief

## Purpose and independence

Create one synthetic, or carefully anonymised and authorised, actuarial or
financial workbook to test Glass Box Excel Audit Agent on a genuinely unseen
case. You must be an independent actuary, accountant or model reviewer. The
developer may provide only this brief, the public formula-support handout and
the blank expected-outcomes template. Do not ask the developer to design,
select or review formulas, planted issues, unusual patterns, output cells or
accounting mismatches before the first run.

## Workbook requirements

The workbook must contain:

- at least five substantive tabs;
- at least 500 formula cells;
- two to five authoritative monetary outputs, identified by exact cell address;
- cross-tab calculations and a mix of straightforward and nested formulas;
- at least one planted control problem inside the published scope;
- at least one formula outside the published support catalogue, retained as a
  valid calculable spreadsheet formula;
- at least one mapping, population-completeness or accounting-context issue;
- at least one unusual but legitimate pattern that should not be reported as an
  error; and
- a quantifiable financial, capital or management consequence.

Use realistic structure and labels, but no live policyholder, employee, client
or confidential production data. Recalculate and save the workbook in Microsoft
Excel or LibreOffice so its formula caches are current. If reference figures are
part of the challenge, provide row-level identifiers, entity, period, currency,
debit/credit orientation, source and a human-confirmed control total. Duplicate
labels must remain distinct rows.

## Expected outcomes and delivery

Complete the expected-outcomes template before delivery. State which issues are
inside the published scope, expected findings and non-findings, unsupported
formulas, authoritative values, expected mapping/population/context results,
separate internal and external verdicts, expected gate stop and expected PDF
availability. Do not choose materiality for the reviewer; describe any result
that depends on threshold as conditional on a human-entered threshold.

Deliver the workbook, reference extract if applicable and completed outcomes to
an independent custodian. The custodian, not the developer, hashes and seals the
exact bytes before the first run. The developer must not manually inspect the
workbook or open the expected outcomes before the continuously recorded run.

## Claim boundary

The exercise measures observable behaviour on one unseen workbook. It does not
validate actuarial methodology, replace professional judgement, certify a
number, provide an audit opinion or establish production scalability. Imperfect
results, including false positives, false negatives and out-of-scope misses,
must be retained and reported honestly.
